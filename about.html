About Engineering College

Training the next generation of engineers and technologists since 2001.

Founded in 2001, Engineering College is a leading institution dedicated to training the next generation of engineers and technologists. Our mission is to combine strong theoretical foundations with hands-on practice, preparing students to solve real problems in a changing world. We value creativity, integrity, teamwork, and lifelong learning.

Our programs

Software Engineering

4 years

Learn to design, build, and test modern software. Covers web development, databases, cloud computing, and agile teamwork.

Computer Science

4 years

Study algorithms, artificial intelligence, data science, and cybersecurity with a strong mathematical base.

Electrical Engineering

4 years

Explore circuits, power systems, electronics, and renewable energy technologies.

Mechanical Engineering

4 years

Master design, thermodynamics, robotics, and manufacturing using modern CAD tools and workshops.

Civil Engineering

4 years

Architecture & Design

5 years
