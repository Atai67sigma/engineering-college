Admissions

Joining Engineering College is simple. Follow the steps below to begin your journey.

Application process

  1. Choose your program. Review our programs and pick the one that fits your goals.
  2. Register online. Fill out the registration form with your details.
  3. Submit documents. Provide your school certificate, ID copy, and a passport-style photo.
  4. Entrance assessment. Take a short test in mathematics and English, followed by a brief interview.
  5. Receive your decision. You will hear from us within 10 working days.
  6. Enroll. Confirm your place by paying the enrollment fee.

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to apply?

A school certificate, a copy of your ID, and a passport-style photo.

Can I study part-time?

Yes. Part-time and online modes are available for most programs.

Is accommodation available?

Yes. Our dormitory offers affordable rooms for out-of-town students.

How do I apply for a scholarship?

Tick the scholarship option during registration and submit your academic results.
